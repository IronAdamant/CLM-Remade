# Loot mode prototype, round 3 (2026-10-11). Second in-game test (debug recorder log + screenshots):
# - Recipes failing a HasPerk condition are not hidden: the workbench lists them greyed out with "Requires <perk>".
#   Only non-perk conditions hide a recipe. The author wants locked legendaries hidden until unlocked.
# - The magazine-style holotape granted its perk but stayed in the inventory anyway.
# So the unlock becomes "carrying the family's holotape": a plain, weightless MISC item, checked with GetItemCount
# (a non-perk condition, so locked recipes stay hidden). No script: works on every platform. The magazine-style
# BOOK and its perk are removed. Conditions are rewritten by loot0c_conditions.py (CTDA bytes written directly).

field.remove | A1ALMMISC_Unlock_Jumpy | . | KSIZ
field.remove | A1ALMMISC_Unlock_Jumpy | Model | MODT
