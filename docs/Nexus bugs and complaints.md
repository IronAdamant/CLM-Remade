# Custom Legendary Modifications: Nexus bugs, issues, and complaints

Handoff for CLM 2.0. Scraped 11 October 2026 from the Nexus page and both trackers.

- Mod: Custom Legendary Modifications, by thingy123 (Deadmansprice)
- Page: https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/5565
- Posts: https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/5565?tab=posts (544 comments, 16 pages, all read)
- Bugs: https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/5565?tab=bugs (16 reports across 2 pages, all read)
- Shipped version: 1.09, 16 July 2017. The author retired in that post and never patched it again.
- Requirements on 1.09: Automatron and Far Harbor. Last build that did not require those DLCs is 0.0025a.
- The Posts badge count of 544 matches 544 unique non-sticky comments. Five stickies repeat on every page.
- The Bugs badge says 4 because only four reports are still marked New. The tracker holds 16 reports in total.
- Forum tab is empty.

Status labels below:

- **Still open.** Reported after 1.09 with no author fix, still marked New on the tracker, or admitted by the author and never walked back.
- **Author said fixed.** He said so in the thread. Many of those were never confirmed by the reporter.
- **Retracted or not this mod.** The reporter or the author closed it as user error, another mod, or install steps.

## Still open

### Bug tracker, still marked New

1. **Jump height and move speed stuck on forever.** lokarmi292, 18 July 2023, issue 712461, version 1.09, 0 replies. "i just been victim from a effect who won't get off and stay in permanence. The guilty: Jump height and movement speed." Same family as Jumpy in 2016, below.
2. **Legendary effects spread onto weapons you did not mod.** SebasSabus, 19 January 2020, issue 438316, version 1.09, 0 replies. An infinite-ammo effect, then a slow-time effect, started appearing on other weapons. The infinite-ammo case happened before teaLz's Legendary Modification was installed. Unanswered.
3. **Cannot talk to Curie in Hole in the Wall.** Geverage9883, 27 December 2021, issue 570587, version 1.09, 0 replies. They uninstalled every other mod and say this is the only one that blocks getting the cure. Unanswered.
4. **Dog armor workbench will not let you exit.** AstroGazer, 29 January 2023, issue 667904, still marked New, but the reporter retracted it: "turned out to be a problem with the k9 armor mod add on from esl convert." Gamepad B / keyboard Tab locked the bench. Vault armor was fine.

### Reported in the posts after 1.09, nobody fixed them

5. **Explosive effects are mini-nuke sized, and "enemy only" bio explosions kill you.** AlkaseltzerBoulevard, 26 November 2025: "the explosive mod is especially bad, causing massive mininuke sized explosions, while the bio-explosions that 'only activate on enemies' ACTUALLY only activates from 5 feet away, guaranteeing you kill yourself." Unanswered. Same class of bug the author failed to fix in 2016.
6. **Vampire only heals on some enemies.** j3lly0m3lly, 26 August 2023. Heals on softshell mirelurks. Does nothing on molerats or radroaches. Unanswered.
7. **Mr. Freeze is a visual only.** MiyaEevee, 30 January 2020. Enemies do not slow down. A 10mm with Mr. Freeze did the same damage as one without it, despite the listed +60 freeze damage. Unanswered.
8. **Time-slow effects never turn off.** SomethingSmith, 23 February 2018. "the time slowing modifications not disabling after their time is up." Unanswered. Pairs with the 2020 report of slow-time spreading onto other guns.
9. **Shotguns lose all spread, on Xbox, including vanilla shotguns.** Randyb7, 20 November 2020. Isolated it by enabling mods one at a time. Load order changes did not help. Unanswered.
10. **Raging on the Disciples blade (Cutlass) drops you to a crawl.** AtomicHobo, 23 August 2020. Movement slows as if overencumbered. They had not tried other blade variants. Unanswered.
11. **Burning Hellish still has "impossible recoil"** after its 75% recoil cut. AidenWatson, 18 June 2018. They wanted it edited to 100% in FO4Edit. GorosMom (9 June 2021) only answered the separate question about stacking two effects.
12. **Deadly Accurate disappeared after an update.** SoulOfSorin, 24 February 2018. Old copies were tagged [CLM]. Unanswered. XxDevilsOutcastxX had already reported on 24 February 2017 that the remake deleted the 100% accuracy / no recoil / 100% range combo, on Xbox as well as PC. The author said that was not intentional and that bugfixes came first. 1.09 added Pinpoint Accuracy and Gunslinger's, not this effect.
13. **Only some CLM effects show up next to Legendary Modification** (mod 4042). Elfykins, 14 July 2018. Unanswered.
14. **Effects never appear on hunted legendary enemies.** AngelicArcher, 14 June 2022. "Ive chosen to hunt for these Legendary effects yet I never see them." Unanswered. The author already said why, on 5 January 2018: "I was planning on adding my legendary effects to the lootlist, but never had time." Delepri (25 May 2023) asked the same thing because a gunner with these effects would one-shot the player. They do not drop. That part was never built.
15. **Almost every effect can be crafted for free, with no requirements.** w0lly13, 3 August 2022. Asked if that was intentional. Unanswered. The holotape has a free/spawn option, so this may be that setting. Nobody confirmed it.
16. **Chemistry bench has no CLM menu, and the plugin has missing masters.** morningair2018, 12 February 2021, Vortex. They have Nuka-World and Far Harbor. "Plug has missing Masters." Ninja2dan asked if they had the DLC. No other reply. 1.09 requires Automatron and Far Harbor. Nuka-World is not the second required master.
17. **Xbox install says something is missing even with both DLCs.** Innadesert420, 13 April 2025. Unanswered.
18. **There is still no no-DLC build.** RazeyBubbles, April and May 2017. The author said a no-DLC edition was too slow to make and he was not going to do it. Last no-DLC file is 0.0025a. 1.09 still requires Automatron and Far Harbor.
19. **The Crafting Framework / Legendary Crafting Framework patches are dead.** DankRafft, 11 November 2025. Both mods in his stickies (May 2019 and July 2020) are gone. He says the replacement is Legendary Effect Overhaul (LEO), https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/66900.
20. **5x Sweetspot stealth field makes human companions refuse to fight.** kenichiwu, 29 August 2017. Companions stealth with you and will not engage. Robots still fight. Removing the CLM gear restored normal combat. They also use Arbitration. The author, 16 December 2017: he reproduced it, spent about three months on real stealth, and "I still have no idea how this can be fixed." He hoped someone else would.
21. **One Punch ragdolls the player and does not work.** drakesteele, 13 March 2017. One of the two One Punch effects was missing from the weapon list. The other ragdolls you as soon as it is on any armor, including power armor, and then does not do what it should. The author said he would look before 1.09. The 1.09 sticky instead says: "One Punch is not possible. I've tried everything. They'll just get harmed when they hit the ground."
22. **Face Bustin' one-shots every non-robot and they explode.** Mikanoshi, 12 March 2017. The author said he would look. 1.09 does not mention a change.
23. **Hazmat-class does not stop rads.** gggg336, 28 February 2017, still broken on 2 March 2017 after an update. The author said "I will fix it. it's my mistake." 1.09 does not mention it. No later confirmation.
24. **Nuka-infused guns fire the wrong bottle.** AgentSmithGSOM, 20 March 2017. Impact effect was right, the projectile was not. 1.09 replaced the bottle with a paddleball and turned those weapons into lobbers so the swap would work. That is a workaround, not the requested projectile.
25. **Second legendary slot does not take these effects.** Only the vanilla legendary keyword is on CLM effects. Leylanee, 5 June 2017: on the Armorsmith Extended 2LM bench, one slot takes CLM and the other only takes vanilla. The author agreed and said he would not make the extra copies. MegaEvilDog, 18 April 2016, had the reverse symptom with 2LM: CLM effects showed only on the second slot. The author said "I don't know how to fix it." GorosMom (2021) says stacking can happen if the item is marked No Legendary.

### Left unfinished in the author's own 1.09 sticky

He wrote these into the final post and then stopped.

- **Silenced Electrical Striker:** range extended. "Don't know if it works for VATS."
- **Critically Hurtin':** "Not much change… I guess it might do something."
- **Advanced Titanium reflect does not work.** Shipped as 5% less damage instead. Blitzkrieg and Bloodlust were dropped for time. "Brutish and Plated Titanium will remain untouched as I don't have time to fix it."
- **Healing another actor does not work.** Healing Touch (Tjonbrown24): "nothing happened, like at all." God Creator and Healing Beams: not possible.
- **Pain Train on armor does nothing.** Tested on bots, deathclaws, and raiders. "Nothing worked out." He had been saying this since June 2016.
- **Disintegration is broken.** Cloak versions crashed. ShouldAttackKill did not ash the body properly. Bodies vanished and left a decal, not an ash pile. He had the same invisibility and "does nothing on a one-hit kill" bugs in March 2016 with Beebww.
- **Loot-list injection was never finished.** Creation Kit crashed whenever he opened Character / Quest to make or edit a quest.
- **Flight was never solved.** To Wickedcircle, 17 December 2017: the Superman-style effects are the best substitute he could find. He never got actual flight working. Heavy and Ze Medic's request got the same answer: use a jetpack.

He also closed these requests as impossible:

- Nature's Wrath (random projectile per shot)
- Stealthy armor with pickpocket at 1,000 feet
- Staminator (no AP drain or AP cost multiplier in the Creation Kit)
- A cinematic kill effect
- Damage scaled to the enemy's remaining health
- Disguises
- A friendly Mirelurk Queen summon
- AgentSmithGSOM's advanced script
- A real range increase beyond lengthening the projectile
- GigaByteWide's request, refused as not in the spirit of the mod

**Game Crashing is intentional.** Leylanee, 5 June 2017: their roommate equipped the "Game crashing" weapon effect and the game crashed. The author confirmed he built that on request.

## Older comment bugs, by what happened to them

These are from 2015 through the February 2017 remake. "Author said fixed" means he said so in the thread. A lot of those were never confirmed by the person who reported them.

### Explosives and self-damage, never really closed

BecauseBotFed, September 2016: Rainman's Cryo and any MIRV-style effect blows you up if line of sight to the first explosion is blocked. Standing on a ledge and aiming down is enough. The child projectiles spawn inside the player. Cryo resist, a cryo-ignore perk, and months of timer, gravity, and seeking tests did not stop it. The author: "Nothing panned out."

dupersaurusrex, February 2017: Wrathful, Fury, High Explosive, and Room Clearing fired missile-sized explosions. Napalmin exploded in your face or in mid-air on automatics and shotguns, and did not work properly on automatic guns. The author swapped in the older incendiary explosions and hoped "ignore LOS checks" would stop the face blasts. dupersaurusrex's 28 February follow-up says Napalmin was still doing it. That is the same complaint AlkaseltzerBoulevard made in November 2025.

### Permanent actor values

dreadziok, 2 to 4 June 2016: Chameleon stayed on after the armor was swapped, including after save and reload. Jumpy jump height stayed after the clothing came off. He had to `setgs fJumpHeightMin` back, and the height reset again every launch. The author guessed `player.setav jumpheightmult 1` and talked about a reset item. No confirmation. lokarmi292 hit the jump-height version again in 2023.

### Armor rating collapses

thenova1991, May and June 2016: adding a CLM effect, Ghostly as the example, dropped a heavy leather chest from 35 armor to 3, and total damage resistance from about 300 to about 18. The author looked in the Creation Kit, found no effect that lowered resistance, and asked which effects did it. No fix confirmed.

### Shotgun Surgeon

CarnackKetral, June and July 2016: on a double-barrel it set ammo capacity to 49, gave machine-gun fire rate, and "the recoil of an elephant gun." The author said the ammo count was intentional so people could try it on rifles. He never answered the fire-rate and recoil report.

### Steady aim breaks scopes

pulot2, 15 to 16 May 2016, including in Far Harbor. Fine at first. Around level 36, short, medium, and long scopes "go out of control." One Steady on a suit, glasses, or mask fails. Combat armor needs only one. Weapon effects that include Steady fail the same way. Two copies still failed after a newer build. Stealth legendaries were "not realy working," with no detail. The author said he would remake Steady the next day. No confirmation.

### Holotape and missing workbench slot, 2016

Several people, and the author could not reproduce it on his 200-mod load order.

- dreadziok: the config changer stuck on the free option and "does not take effect."
- butthead123: holotape set to free, and the legendary slot never appears.
- hummus23: same stuck-free bug after a manual reinstall and a load-order move.
- Decadurabolin: vanilla benches show no legendary entry.
- ElderichKnight, April 2016, NMM: no error on install, no legendary mods at the armor bench, and on an already-legendary piece the CLM line flashes and vanishes. The author guessed a keyword overflow.
- Granverion got a green check in NMM only after repeated reinstalls.

butthead123 and hummus23 never confirmed a fix. gggg336's advice was to also install teaLz's mod and load CLM near the bottom.

### Plugin will not load

isnehandlampe and Paragod911, May 2016: current esp would not load. An older file, "2.5d," did. Petrus7: the file was red because they did not own Far Harbor or Automatron. The author: after 0.0025a those two DLCs are mandatory. "there is no way around it."

### Covert Ops was loud

AlCahpwn, May 2016: through 0.0025b the suppressor keyword was missing, so the gun stayed loud without a suppressor. Other stats worked. Murderously Silent and Deathly Silenced did silence a gun. The author: "I may have fucked up." The user then confirmed silence worked. The description still did not say silenced. A "harder to detect" effect on a weapon "never worked" for the author and is armor-only.

### Forcible's fire rate

jonathanprice, 17 April 2016: the text says fire rate is reduced by 30%. It sets fire rate to 30% of normal. Author: fixed for the next version.

### Slow Aim does not work. Burning Rage was wrong.

Author, 8 May 2016, while asking Cidion77 to test Flame Cloak. Burning Rage "will be fixed." No version number and no confirmation. Slow Aim was left as not working.

### Alien Tech

SphereOfRa, 22 May 2016: the Tesla arc caps the range and the lightning visual is annoying. The author replaced it. No user confirmation.

### Havoc's red laser

The author called the Liberty Prime explosion a known failure, then on 5 April 2016 said Granverion's Assaultron-head-laser suggestion fixed it.

### Performance Centre fire rate never stayed right

- harveeymon, December 2015: no fire-rate increase on the SMG, .44, or pipe revolver, and the requested limb damage was missing.
- Kirkinshnifel, January 2016: the listed 30% behaved like a huge jump, about 110 to 272. Author said fixed.
- gggg336, 1 March 2016: it makes any gun's fire rate very slow. The author said he would retest. No confirmation.

### Stealth and invisibility

- captainn9ne, December 2015: Chameleon hides the Pip-Boy while you sneak, so you have to stand up to use the map. Left for the GECK.
- ronnie20, January 2016: Ghost flickers near NPCs and they still see you in the dark. The author declared it fixed, then on 1 February 2016 said Ghost still had no success.
- Sportsdude, March 2016: Camouflaged and Ghostly were always on. The author added an IsSneaking condition and said both were fixed. He also said the Ghostly and Camouflage descriptions were misleading. None of those reporters confirmed the fix.

### Toxic Blood does nothing

Author, January through February 2016, all of his tests. Uploaded a test copy in 0.0012 anyway. "No matter what i did, the toxic blood effects were not working."

### Disintegration, March 2016, with Beebww

One-shot everything, sometimes played plasma-goo and disintegration together, turned living targets invisible for a few seconds, left corpses that could be hit again to replay it, and did not ash a one-hit kill. Liberty Prime still one-shot after the other colors stopped. Matching fDelay and fDelayEnd caused either permanent invisibility or an instant kill. The author: "Everything's working except the effects do not activate when you instantly kill an enemy," and bodies still went invisible. He said a real death trigger needed the Creation Kit. The 1.09 sticky says he still had not solved it.

### Vampiric life-on-hit

Missed lasers, then worked on rifles only for single shots and not automatics. One test against an invulnerable turret killed the author. He later said he removed the conditions and it was fulfilled. Stormline09 never confirmed. This is a different failure from the 2023 "only some enemies" report, which is on the final version.

### Damage multipliers ignored pure energy weapons

daemon976, 16 March 2016: Savage, Zeus-Crafted, and the other multipliers worked on ballistic guns only. The author had not set dtEnergy. He said he added it, plus Harsh Radioactive, Achlys' Poisoning, and Zeus' Shocking. No confirmation. Zeus-Crafted dealing fire was intentional. The electrical version was added beside it.

### Savage accuracy

juv3nal, February 2016: accuracy at the bench dropped versus Zeus-Crafted, and live fire was scattered. The author zeroed the aim-model recoil and cone floats (AimModelRecoilArcDeg, AimModelRecoilArcRotateDeg, AimModelConeIncreasePerShot, AimModelRecoilMaxDegPerShot). juv3nal confirmed he could "paint a happy face" with bullet impacts.

### Enlightened did not raise Agility

CarnackKetral, 22 February 2016. It gave 5 AP and 15 damage resist instead of +2 to all SPECIAL. The author added Agility in 0.0015c and did not mention the other stats. No confirmation.

### Heavy Impactin heals too fast

blm1123581321, 22 September 2016. Nearly unkillable unless one-shot. They wanted the impact without the heal. The author said that should not happen and that he would look during the remake. No confirmation.

### Rechargeable ammo cannot have a magazine

keltas, June 2016. Ammo type NONE worked. `iammocapacity` did not, so it never reloads and "destroys everything in sight like a gatling laser." The author shipped that on purpose as Flawed Factorium.

### Chain projectiles only work on beams

Author, June 2016. Blue laser, red laser, and Gauss work. Alien, cryo, and plasma show a muzzle flash and no projectile. Missile, lobber, and arrow did not function. He expected cone, flame, and barrier to fail the same way.

### Jedi Reflexes / Bouncin'

Block-triggered reflect did not work through January 2016. Reflect was permanent instead. Only lasers showed a deflect visual. Missiles and grenades still hurt you. A Cavalier-plus-ricochet tie was "not possible." The author later said the block condition worked, and kookamango on 22 May 2016 said the Jedi build "works perfectly." Missiles and grenades were never claimed fixed.

### Other author "this does not work" notes from the early months

- Beginner lost its 5th property, accuracy, because FO4Edit would not save it.
- Gunslinger the perk was overwritten by a leftover test record and clashed with Reperkification. The author said the next upload stopped touching it.
- No Brakes could not attach an explosion to Pain Train.
- Birdshot's 10 pellets, Hyperion-style reverse recoil, quad shot, 0.1 ammo per shot, and limiting an effect to Gatling lasers, X-01, arm pieces, or the Mr Handy buzz saw were all called impossible in FO4Edit.
- Heavy Impact's power-armor landing could not be made. Explosive vent was the stand-in.
- Luck of the Draw and Thermal Capacitor waited on the Creation Kit and are not shown as finished.
- Electric-shield visuals could not be made. The fire shield worked.
- Saitama melee tests could disintegrate the player.
- Pre-Suppressed shipped untested. ronnie20 said suppression worked.
- Stealth's constructible object used the wrong form ID. Razakel fixed it locally and the author reuploaded.
- Made by Zeus and Unbreakable shipped with "Request fulfilled. Untested."
- The Wind / Majestic Swagger had knockback mistakes the author said he fixed.
- Crippingly Rapid console IDs only work if the load-order index matches. Erierth confirmed that.
- Armorsmith Extended looked incompatible until MasterZeh crafted at the vanilla bench. Order that worked: Legendary Modifications, then CLM, then Armorsmith.
- Bobydoo69, March 2016: "most of these mods make the projectiles invisible." The author said he had no projectile overrides and asked which effects. No answer.
- Pradidye, April 2016: removing the mod from the load order caused a startup crash. The author asked whether the custom-legendary items were removed first. No reply.

The posts also include a large number of feature requests the author either built, refused, or left on the Google docs linked from the mod page. Those are not defect reports unless one of the entries above already covers them.
