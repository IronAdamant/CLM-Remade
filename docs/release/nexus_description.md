# Custom Legendary Modifications Remade 2.0

*Over 570 legendary effects for weapons and armour - craft them, or hunt for them.*

## What it is

CLM adds hundreds of legendary modifications you attach at a weapons or armour workbench: elemental hits, spawned allies and enemies, grenade and mine launchers, coloured lasers, movement and stealth effects, and some that are gloriously overpowered. Simple settings decide what you get: everything free, a costly crafting game, balanced legendaries only, or a loot hunt - and a picker to show only the legendaries you want.

## What's new in 2.0

CLM was last updated in 2017. 2.0 is a full overhaul:

- **Four DLC instead of six:** Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World. Wasteland Workshop and Vault-Tec Workshop are no longer needed.
- **Loot mode.** The old "No-craft version / WIP" option, finished: legendaries drop as loose mods from anything you kill (vanilla, DLC or modded enemies), starred legendaries drop far more, family holotapes unlock crafting, and OP legendaries need a rare Legendary Core. Drop rate: Low, Standard or Higher.
- **15 new legendaries** built from pieces 1.09 never finished: Lobbin' mines, Hurtin' (1000%), Instant Killin', Medic's, Overchargin', VATS Penetratin', Quick Hands, Marathon Runnin', Focused, Stealth Boy'd and more.
- **Coloured lasers:** Green, Purple, Gold, Orange, Pink and Deep Blue, with matching beams, muzzle flashes, sparks and light.
- **Dozens of fixes:** legendaries that never worked now do, effects match their descriptions, free recipes that should have had a cost, and holotape menu bugs.
- **Settings that work everywhere:** made at any chemistry station, so PlayStation players get the full mod setup for the first time (see below).
- **Legendary Picker:** too many legendaries at the workbench? Pick the families you want at the chemistry station and see only those.

## Requirements

- Fallout 4 with four DLC: **Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World**.
- No script extender (F4SE) needed. Works on the current game and on older versions (1.10.163 and earlier) - no need to unpack the archives.

## Installing

Use a mod manager (Vortex or Mod Organizer 2). The installer asks: **packed archives** (recommended, any PC game version) or **loose files**, and the optional **F4SE menu**.

## Getting started

Nothing to set up: a new game starts with Costly crafting and balanced legendaries (no OP ones, no variants). Attach legendaries in the "Legendary" slot of any weapons or armour workbench.

## Settings and the chemistry station - please read

CLM's settings are **items**, not menu switches:

- Every setting is a weightless **"CLM Setting: ..."** item in your Pip-Boy's Misc tab. **Carry it = on. Drop it (or store or sell it) = off.** Carry none = the defaults.
- Make them for free at any **chemistry station**, category **"CLM Settings"**: Free crafting, Loot mode, Show OP legendaries, Show variants, Hide perk-style / spawning legendaries, Picker mode, and the Low / Higher drop rate (loot mode).
- A setting you already carry is not offered again. Free crafting and Loot mode replace each other.
- PC and Xbox: the **(CLM) Configuration Holotape** (given when you leave Vault 111) gives and takes the same items from your Pip-Boy - use whichever you like.
- The chemistry station and workbench don't refresh while open: after a change, leave and use them again.
- Something missing? Check your Misc tab - Loot mode hides crafting and the picker, and an item you already carry hides its own recipe. Armour legendaries are at the **armour** workbench, on pieces with a legendary slot (not clothing such as the Vault suit).

## Legendary Picker

Make **"CLM Setting: Picker mode"** and the workbench shows only the families you pick. At the chemistry station, open a **CLM Armour**, **CLM Spawns** or **CLM Weapon** category and make a pick (e.g. CLM Weapon: Damage > "CLM Pick: Hurtin' (Weapon)"). Drop a pick to hide that family again; drop Picker mode to see everything. Your other settings (OP, variants) still apply; OP-only families are also under **CLM OP**.

## Optional F4SE menu (PC)

Have **F4SE** and **Mod Configuration Menu**? Tick **CLM - MCM** in the installer and set everything from the pause menu: crafting mode, drop rate, OP legendaries, variants, the Legendary Picker - plus pages to add or remove any of the perk holotape's 110 perks. Nothing to carry: no setting or pick items, no holotapes (turn "Holotapes" on in the menu if you want them). Before removing it, press "Turn settings and picks back into items" in the menu.

## Why the chemistry station?

In 1.09 the settings were holotape menus run by scripts. PlayStation mods can't use custom scripts, so on PS4 part of the mod couldn't be set up at all. Settings crafted at a chemistry station need no scripts, so 2.0 works the same on PC, Xbox and PlayStation. The chemistry station is the way for everyone without a script extender - Xbox and PlayStation players, and PC players who don't use F4SE. PC players with F4SE can use the optional menu instead (above).

## PlayStation version

CLM 2.0 is also on Bethesda.net for PS4/PS5. PlayStation mods cannot use scripts or new assets, so there:

- Settings are made at the chemistry station (no holotapes); loot mode also needs the CLM Loot Manual, made once there.
- The new laser colours and the Kellogg spawn are left out.
- Vampiric and its family heal on every hit except killing blows; Auto-Medical and Aid Patch regenerate health instead of using stimpaks; Face Bustin' does not blow targets apart.
- Untouchable, Shavka's Explosive Striking, Ridiculously Absorbin' and Sadistic Brawlers do nothing there; a few others lose one scripted part.

## Compatibility

CLM changes no vanilla records, so it works alongside most mods; loot mode works with enemies from other mods too.

- **Legendary Effect Overhaul (LEO):** compatible, any load order (checked with LEO 2.1.3). CLM's legendaries use the normal legendary slot (LEO's "L1"); LEO's extra slots are for LEO's own effects. Both loot systems can run together.
- Old Crafting Framework patches made for 1.09 aren't needed and no longer apply.

## Upgrading from 1.09

Your gear keeps its legendaries, DLC ones included, and your holotape settings are turned into setting items on the first load.
