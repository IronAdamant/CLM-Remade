Custom Legendary Modifications Remade 2.0

Over 570 legendary effects for weapons and armour - craft them, or hunt for them.

What it is

CLM adds hundreds of legendary modifications you attach at a weapons or armour workbench: elemental hits, spawned allies and enemies, grenade and mine launchers, coloured lasers, movement and stealth effects, and some that are gloriously overpowered. Simple settings decide what you get: everything free, a costly crafting game, balanced legendaries only, or a loot hunt - and a picker to show only the legendaries you want.

What's new in 2.0

CLM was last updated in 2017. 2.0 is a full overhaul:

  • Four DLC instead of six: Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World. Wasteland Workshop and Vault-Tec Workshop are no longer needed.
  • Loot mode. The old "No-craft version / WIP" option, finished: legendaries drop as loose mods from anything you kill (vanilla, DLC or modded enemies), starred legendaries drop far more, family holotapes unlock crafting, and OP legendaries need a rare Legendary Core. Drop rate: Low, Standard or Higher.
  • 15 new legendaries built from pieces 1.09 never finished: Lobbin' mines, Hurtin' (1000%), Instant Killin', Medic's, Overchargin', VATS Penetratin', Quick Hands, Marathon Runnin', Focused, Stealth Boy'd and more.
  • Coloured lasers: Green, Purple, Gold, Orange, Pink and Deep Blue, with matching beams, muzzle flashes, sparks and light.
  • Dozens of fixes: legendaries that never worked now do, effects match their descriptions, free recipes that should have had a cost, and holotape menu bugs.
  • Settings that work everywhere: made at any chemistry station, so PlayStation players get the full mod setup for the first time (see below).
  • Legendary Picker: too many legendaries at the workbench? Pick the families you want at the chemistry station and see only those.

Requirements

  • Fallout 4 with four DLC: Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World.
  • No script extender (F4SE) needed. Works on the current game and on older versions (1.10.163 and earlier) - no need to unpack the archives.

Installing

Use a mod manager (Vortex or Mod Organizer 2). The installer asks: packed archives (recommended, any PC game version) or loose files, and the optional F4SE menu.

Getting started

Nothing to set up. A new game starts with costly crafting, balanced legendaries (no OP ones), the main variants, and the concise view (one legendary per group of similar effects). Attach legendaries in the "Legendary" slot of any weapons or armour workbench.

Settings - please read

Settings decide two things: how you get legendaries and what the workbench shows. They are items, not menu switches:

  • Every setting is a weightless "CLM Setting: ..." item in your Pip-Boy's Misc tab. Carry it = on. Drop it (or store or sell it) = off. Carry none = the defaults.
  • Make them for free at any chemistry station, category "CLM Settings". PC and Xbox: the (CLM) Configuration Holotape (given when you leave Vault 111) gives and takes the same items. PC with F4SE: the optional menu (below).

How you get legendaries

  • Free crafting: legendaries cost nothing. Without it: they cost components (costly). Replaces Loot mode.
  • Loot mode: legendaries drop from the enemies you kill instead of being crafted (see Loot mode below). Replaces Free crafting.
  • Low drop rate / Higher drop rate (loot mode): with neither, Standard.
  • Hidden legendaries drop too (loot mode): the legendaries the concise view hides can drop as well.

What the workbench shows

  • Show all legendaries: every legendary, as before 2.1. Without it: the concise view (below).
  • CLM Group: ... (category CLM Groups): shows one whole group of the concise view.
  • Show OP legendaries: the overpowered ones too. Without it: balanced only.
  • Show variants: every strength of each legendary (Jumpy 10% to 300%...). Without it: the main ones.
  • Hide perk-style legendaries: without it, they are shown.
  • Show spawning legendaries: the legendaries that spawn creatures and people. Without it (and without Show all legendaries) they are hidden, so a new game starts with weapons and armour only. ("Hide spawning legendaries" still hides them even with Show all.)
  • Picker mode: only the families you pick (see Legendary Picker below). Without it: every family.

Good to know:

  • A setting you already carry is not offered again. Free crafting and Loot mode replace each other, and so do the two drop rates.
  • The chemistry station and workbench don't refresh while open: after a change, leave and use them again.
  • Something missing? Check your Misc tab: a setting may be hiding it, or you already carry the item. Armour legendaries are at the armour workbench, on pieces with a legendary slot (not clothing such as the Vault suit).

The concise view

Many legendaries feel alike, so the workbench shows one balanced legendary per group of similar effects - 33 groups, such as "More damage" (Hurtin' (50%) shows; Hurtin' (25%), Hurtin' (100%), Devastating and Beginner wait in the group) or "Grenades and mines". Where no single legendary stood for its group, a new one does: Opportunist's, Elemental Strikin', Double Tappin', Resistance (All), Vitality, Untouchable (Elements), Radiatin' (Elements). Spawns show each creature's plain Random spawn; the Legendary and Boss spawns are a group of their own.

  • Nothing is removed. Gear that has a hidden legendary keeps it.
  • Make "CLM Setting: Show all legendaries" to see every legendary again, or a "CLM Group" item (chemistry station, category CLM Groups) to see one whole group. Each group item lists what it adds.
  • Variants and OP legendaries are not part of it: Show variants and Show OP legendaries handle those.
  • Saves made before 2.1 keep everything shown: they get "Show all legendaries" when you load them (PC and Xbox). On PlayStation, make it at the chemistry station.

Loot mode

  • Legendaries drop from any enemy you kill (vanilla, DLC or from other mods) as loose mods: attach one at a workbench and it is used up instead of components. Starred legendary enemies drop far more.
  • CLM holotapes drop too, one per family: carry one to craft its whole family. OP legendaries also need a Legendary Core (weapon or armour), a rare drop.
  • Standard drop rate, per kill: common 3%, uncommon 1%, rare 0.3%, OP 0.1%.
  • The legendaries the concise view hides don't drop unless Hidden legendaries drop too (or Show all legendaries) is on.

Legendary Picker

Make "CLM Setting: Picker mode" and the workbench shows only the families you pick. At the chemistry station, open a CLM Armour, CLM Spawns or CLM Weapon category and make a pick (e.g. CLM Weapon: Damage > "CLM Pick: Hurtin' (Weapon)"). Drop a pick to hide that family again; drop Picker mode to see every family. The other settings still apply on top (OP, variants, the concise view); OP-only families are also under CLM OP.

Optional F4SE menu (PC)

Have F4SE and Mod Configuration Menu? Tick CLM - MCM in the installer and set everything from the pause menu: the Settings page (crafting mode, drop rate, hidden drops, Show all, OP, variants, Picker mode), the Legendary Picker, a Concise view page with a switch per group, and pages to add or remove any of the perk holotape's 110 perks. Nothing to carry: no setting, pick or group items, no holotapes (turn "Holotapes" on in the menu if you want them). Before removing it, press "Turn settings and picks back into items" in the menu.

Why the chemistry station?

In 1.09 the settings were holotape menus run by scripts. PlayStation mods can't use custom scripts, so on PS4 part of the mod couldn't be set up at all. Settings crafted at a chemistry station need no scripts, so 2.0 works the same on PC, Xbox and PlayStation. The chemistry station is the way for everyone without a script extender - Xbox and PlayStation players, and PC players who don't use F4SE. PC players with F4SE can use the optional menu instead (above).

PlayStation version

CLM 2.0 is also on Bethesda.net for PS4/PS5. PlayStation mods cannot use scripts or new assets, so there:

  • Settings are made at the chemistry station (no holotapes); loot mode also needs the CLM Loot Manual, made once there. CLM's perks are "CLM Perk" magazines there too (category CLM Perks): reading one adds the perk.
  • Saves made before 2.1: make "CLM Setting: Show all legendaries" to keep every legendary shown.
  • The new laser colours and the Kellogg spawn are left out.
  • Vampiric and its family heal on every hit except killing blows; Auto-Medical and Aid Patch regenerate health instead of using stimpaks; Face Bustin' does not blow targets apart.
  • Untouchable, Shavka's Explosive Striking, Ridiculously Absorbin' and Sadistic Brawlers do nothing there; a few others lose one scripted part.

Compatibility

CLM changes no vanilla records, so it works alongside most mods; loot mode works with enemies from other mods too.

  • Legendary Effect Overhaul (LEO): compatible, any load order (checked with LEO 2.1.3). CLM's legendaries use the normal legendary slot (LEO's "L1"); LEO's extra slots are for LEO's own effects. Both loot systems can run together.
  • Old Crafting Framework patches made for 1.09 aren't needed and no longer apply.

Upgrading

Your gear keeps its legendaries, DLC ones included, and your holotape settings are turned into setting items on the first load. From 2.0: everything stays shown (you get "Show all legendaries"); some very strong legendaries are now OP, so make "Show OP legendaries" to keep crafting them.
