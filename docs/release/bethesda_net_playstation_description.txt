CUSTOM LEGENDARY MODIFICATIONS REMADE 2.1 (PLAYSTATION)

Over 600 legendary effects for weapons and armour. Craft them at the workbench, or hunt for them as loot.

WHAT'S NEW IN 2.1
- Concise view: the workbench shows one balanced legendary per group of similar effects (82 instead of 316). Nothing is removed.
- Over 70 new legendaries, new armour effects among them.
- Rebalanced: 56 very strong legendaries moved to OP; weak ones were brought up.
- Spawning legendaries are hidden by default. Reading a CLM Perk magazine no longer crashes.

REQUIREMENTS
Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World.

GETTING STARTED
Nothing to set up. Attach legendaries in the "Legendary" slot of a weapons or armour workbench (armour with a legendary slot).

SETTINGS (please read)
Settings are free "CLM Setting" items made at any chemistry station (CLM Settings). Carry one = on; drop, store or sell it = off.
- How you get legendaries: Free crafting, Loot mode, drop rates.
- What the workbench shows: Show all legendaries, Show OP legendaries, Show variants, Show spawning legendaries, Picker mode and more.
After a change, leave the workbench and use it again.
Loot mode: also make and read the "CLM Loot Manual" once.

PLAYSTATION DIFFERENCES
PlayStation mods can't use scripts or new art, so:
- No holotapes. CLM perks are "CLM Perk" magazines (chemistry station).
- Left out: the new laser colours and the Kellogg spawn.
- Changed: Vampiric's family heals on hits but not kills; Auto-Medical and Aid Patch regenerate health; BioReactive explodes on kills only; Midas' leaves ordinary ash.
- Some effects that need a script do nothing or lose a part. Their descriptions say so.

UPGRADING
Your gear keeps its legendaries. Saves from before 2.1: make "Show all legendaries" to keep everything shown, and "Show OP legendaries" to keep crafting those now OP.
