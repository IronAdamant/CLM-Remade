CUSTOM LEGENDARY MODIFICATIONS REMADE 2.0

Over 570 legendary effects for weapons and armour. Craft them at the workbench, or hunt for them as loot. Elemental hits, spawned allies and enemies, grenade and mine launchers, coloured lasers, movement and stealth effects, and some gloriously overpowered ones.

WHAT'S NEW IN 2.0
CLM was last updated in 2017. 2.0 is a full overhaul:
- Loot mode: legendaries drop from anything you kill. Holotapes unlock whole families for crafting, and OP legendaries need a rare Legendary Core. Drop rate: Low, Standard or Higher.
- Legendary Picker: the workbench shows only the legendary families you pick.
- 15 new legendaries and six new laser colours.
- Dozens of fixes: broken legendaries now work, and effects match their descriptions.

REQUIREMENTS
Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World. Wasteland Workshop and Vault-Tec Workshop are no longer needed.
Why: most players own the DLC by now, and one plugin is much easier for me to keep updated.

GETTING STARTED
Nothing to set up. A new game starts with costly crafting, balanced legendaries (no OP ones), the main variants and the concise view.
Attach legendaries in the "Legendary" slot of a weapons or armour workbench. Armour legendaries need armour with a legendary slot, not clothing like the Vault suit.

SETTINGS (please read)
Settings are free "CLM Setting" items made at any chemistry station (CLM Settings). Carry one = on; drop, store or sell it = off; carry none = the defaults. Or use the Configuration Holotape, given when you leave Vault 111.
- How you get legendaries: Free crafting, Loot mode, Low / Higher drop rate, Hidden legendaries drop too.
- What the workbench shows: Show all legendaries, Show OP legendaries, Show variants, Hide perk-style legendaries, Show spawning legendaries (hidden by default), Picker mode.
After a change, leave the workbench and use it again. Something missing? Check your Misc tab.

CONCISE VIEW (NEW IN 2.1)
The workbench shows one balanced legendary per group of similar effects (33 groups). Nothing is removed. "Show all legendaries" shows everything; a "CLM Group" item (CLM Groups) shows one group. Saves from before 2.1 get "Show all legendaries" automatically.

UPGRADING
Your gear keeps its legendaries, and your holotape settings become setting items on the first load. Some very strong legendaries are now OP: make "Show OP legendaries" to keep crafting them.

COMPATIBILITY
CLM changes no vanilla records, so it works alongside most mods, including Legendary Effect Overhaul (LEO).
