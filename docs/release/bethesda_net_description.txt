CUSTOM LEGENDARY MODIFICATIONS REMADE 2.1

Over 650 legendary effects for weapons and armour. Craft them at the workbench, or hunt for them as loot.

WHAT'S NEW IN 2.1
- Concise view: the workbench shows one balanced legendary per group of similar effects (82 instead of 316). Nothing is removed.
- About 90 new legendaries: new armour effects, the lost 2016 legendaries, and requests once called impossible (CQC, DragonBlade, Necromancer's...).
- Rebalanced: 56 very strong legendaries moved to OP; weak ones were brought up.
- Many fixes. Spawning legendaries are hidden by default.

REQUIREMENTS
Automatron, Far Harbor, Contraptions Workshop and Nuka-World.

GETTING STARTED
Nothing to set up. Attach legendaries in the "Legendary" slot of a weapons or armour workbench (armour with a legendary slot, not clothing).

SETTINGS (please read)
Settings are free "CLM Setting" items made at any chemistry station (CLM Settings). Carry one = on; drop, store or sell it = off. Or use the Configuration Holotape, given when you leave Vault 111.
- How you get legendaries: Free crafting, Loot mode, drop rates.
- What the workbench shows: Show all legendaries, Show OP legendaries, Show variants, Show spawning legendaries, Picker mode and more.
After a change, leave the workbench and use it again. Something missing? Check your Misc tab.

UPGRADING
Your gear keeps its legendaries. Saves from before 2.1 get "Show all legendaries", so nothing disappears. To keep crafting the legendaries now OP, make "Show OP legendaries".

COMPATIBILITY
CLM changes no vanilla records: it works with most mods, including Legendary Effect Overhaul (LEO).
